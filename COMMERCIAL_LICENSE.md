# Clipwell Commercial License Framework

> **Draft only — not an offer, not a binding license, and not legal advice.** This framework must be completed with the seller’s identity, commercial scope, pricing, jurisdiction, privacy terms, and legal review before it is presented to customers or used to accept payment.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

A future Clipwell commercial agreement may govern original Clipwell services, support, original add-ons, or other material specifically identified in an order form or commercial schedule (the **Commercial Materials**). It does not revoke, narrow, or replace the MIT License that applies to the public Clipwell codebase and any included MIT-covered components.

The agreement must identify the exact Commercial Materials, their delivery method, the customer’s permitted use, and any support term. A commercial purchase must never be described as conferring exclusive rights to MIT-covered code.

## 2. MIT-Covered Components

The Clipwell public release is distributed under the MIT License in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE), and it preserves upstream attribution in [`NOTICE.md`](./NOTICE.md). Copyright and permission notices for those components must remain included in copies or substantial portions of MIT-covered material.

Nothing in a future commercial agreement may prohibit a recipient from exercising rights already granted under the MIT License for the MIT-covered portions of Clipwell.

## 3. Commercial Grant — To Be Finalized

Subject to payment and the final order form, the seller may grant the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the identified Commercial Materials for the customer’s internal business purposes during the stated term. This clause must be finalized only after the seller defines the paid product, territory, user or device limits, redistribution policy, and any hosted-service access rules.

## 4. Support, Updates, and Service Levels — To Be Finalized

If a commercial offer includes support or updates, the final agreement must state the support channel, covered versions, response targets, support hours, maintenance term, exclusions, and any suspension or end-of-life policy. No service-level commitment exists until those terms are explicitly published or agreed in writing.

## 5. Fees, Taxes, Refunds, and Renewal — To Be Finalized

The final order form must state the price, currency, payment processor, applicable taxes, renewal mechanics, cancellation process, and refund policy. The website must not display a purchase CTA until these terms are available and legally reviewed for the seller’s jurisdiction and target customers.

## 6. Data and Privacy

The current public Clipwell app is local-first and does not require an account. Any future commercial service that processes customer, payment, device, or usage data must publish a current privacy notice and identify the data controller, retention period, processors, security measures, and applicable user rights before collection begins.

## 7. Warranty, Liability, and Legal Review

MIT-covered material retains the MIT License’s disclaimer. Any separate commercial warranty, indemnity, liability limit, governing law, dispute procedure, or export-control term requires jurisdiction-specific legal review and must be written into the final agreement.

## 8. Current Status

There is no active Clipwell commercial license today. The current public product remains available under the MIT License. For the commercialization roadmap and required decisions, see [`docs/COMMERCIALIZATION.md`](./docs/COMMERCIALIZATION.md).
